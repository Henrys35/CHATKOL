# 英文模板（CHATKOL）

> 看板用到这里的三段：「跟进一」「跟进二」是自动跟进发的信，「私信 · 通用版」是「私信待发」清单里复制用的模板。「首封」看板不用，给你在 EasyKOL 或邮箱里发第一封时参考。名字写成 `{博主昵称}`，看板会换成对方的名字。每段第一个代码块是正文。现在是一个虚构的居家健身 App「Acme Fit」的版本，当例子用，安装时按你的产品改写。
## 首封

```
Hi {博主昵称},

[One line about a specific video of theirs.]

I'm Alex, Partnerships Lead at Acme Fit, a home-workout app that builds a 20-minute plan around your schedule and the equipment you have.

I'd love to do one paid video with you that feels like your normal content. You come up with the idea and write the script; we only check the product details before you film.

What we have in mind:
• One short video, under 60 seconds, on your main account
• Posted within the next couple of weeks
• A free year of Acme Fit Premium for you

If you're open to it, what would your rate be for one video?

No pressure at all, and thanks for reading!

Best,
Alex
Partnerships Lead, Acme Fit
```

## 跟进一

```
Hi {博主昵称},

Just floating this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried, I know how busy things get!

We'd love to do one paid video with you on your main account. You'd have full creative control over the idea and script, and we'd only double-check the product details before you film.

If that sounds fun, what would your rate be for one video? And if now isn't the right time, totally fine. Thanks either way!

Best,
{发件人}
```

## 跟进二

```
Hi {博主昵称},

I'll keep this short so I don't keep filling your inbox. This is my last note for now.

If a paid collab ever fits your plans, just reply with your rate for one video and I'll take it from there. Either way, I really enjoy what you share. Keep it up!

Best,
{发件人}
```

```
Acme Fit is a home-workout app that builds a 20-minute plan around your schedule and equipment. Would you be open to one paid video on your main account? The idea and script are all yours.

If it sounds interesting, what's your rate for one video? Happy to share details!
```
